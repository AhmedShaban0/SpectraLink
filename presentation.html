Chapter 4 · The Discontinuity

The 2012 Climate Trap: The Fake 404% Surge

What happens if you simply plot raw satellite data from 2001 to 2025 without harmonizing?

⚠️ The 4.04x Artifact

In January 2012, recorded fire hot spots suddenly jumped by +404%.

Did fires across the globe actually quadruple overnight? NO! The satellite camera resolution upgraded from 1km to 375m!

Why This Ruined Climate Science:

  • Comparing a 2005 fire to a 2018 fire was like comparing apples to watermelons.
  • Climate models falsely predicted catastrophic fire surges.
  • Summing both satellites double-counted identical fires.
